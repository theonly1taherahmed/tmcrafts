How do I reset a game's progress?

To reset a game's progress, look for a settings or options menu within the game. Many games have a "Reset Progress" or "Clear Data" option. If you can't find it, try clearing your browser cache for our website, but note that this will reset all games' progress.

Why are the games not loading properly?

If games aren't loading, try these steps:

  1. Refresh the page
  2. Clear your browser cache and cookies
  3. Check your internet connection
  4. Try using a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
  5. Disable browser extensions that might interfere

If problems persist, contact our support team with details about your device and browser.

Are there any age restrictions for playing games on tm crafts?

tm crafts is designed to be family-friendly and suitable for all ages. However, we recommend that children under 13 use the site with parental supervision. Some games might require basic reading skills or involve challenges that are more suitable for older children.

Why is a game running slowly on my device?

Game performance can be affected by several factors:

  • Your device's processing power and memory
  • Other applications running in the background
  • Internet connection speed
  • Browser capabilities

Try closing other tabs and applications, and make sure your browser is updated to the latest version.

What information does tm crafts collect?

We collect minimal information to improve your experience. This may include:

  • Non-personal information (browser type, device type)
  • Game progress and scores (if you choose to save them)
  • Anonymous usage statistics to improve our games

We do not collect personal information without your consent. For more details, please read our Privacy Policy.

How often are new games added?

We add new games regularly, typically 2-3 new games per month. We also update existing games with new levels and features based on user feedback. Follow us on social media to get notified about new releases!

Can I play tm crafts games on my mobile device?

Yes! Most of our games are mobile-friendly and can be played on smartphones and tablets. For the best experience, we recommend using a modern browser like Chrome or Safari on your mobile device. Some games might work better in landscape orientation.

Can I suggest a new game idea?

Absolutely! We love hearing game ideas from our community. Please use our contact form to share your suggestions. While we can't implement every idea, we consider all suggestions and often create games based on user requests.
