Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset a game's progress?

To reset a game's progress, look for a settings or options menu within the game. Many games have a "Reset Progress" or "Clear Data" option. If you can't find it, try clearing your browser cache for our website.

Why are the games not loading properly?

If games aren't loading, try these steps: 1) Refresh the page, 2) Clear your browser cache, 3) Check your internet connection, 4) Try using a different browser. If problems persist, contact us with details about your device and browser.

How can I suggest a new game or feature?

We love hearing your suggestions! Please use the contact form above to share your ideas for new games or features. Be as detailed as possible about what you'd like to see.

Are my quiz results saved?

Quiz results are typically stored in your browser's local storage. This means they will be saved on your device but won't be accessible from other devices or browsers. Clearing your browser data will remove these results.
